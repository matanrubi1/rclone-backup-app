Privacy policy

rclone-backup is used by a single person, its owner, to back up the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • The Google Drive access it requests is used only to read, write and list the owner's backup files in the owner's own Drive.
  • No data is collected, stored, shared, sold or transferred to any third party. Nothing leaves the owner's computer except the owner's files, which go directly to the owner's own Google Drive.
  • There are no other users, no analytics and no advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
